One Telugu heritage

Two states, one shared culture

Telangana and Andhra Pradesh share the Telugu language and literature, customs, performing arts, food and hospitality. Each state has its own regional expression of that heritage.

  • Telangana

    Deccan and Kakatiya heritage

    A south-central state with a Deccan landscape, Hyderabad, rich folk traditions, Bathukamma and Bonalu.

  • Andhra Pradesh

    Coastal and Buddhist heritage

    A southeastern state with a long Bay of Bengal coastline, temple and Buddhist heritage, Kuchipudi and Sankranti.

Festivals

Celebrations through the year

Bathukamma and Bonalu are strongly identified with Telangana. Sankranti and Ugadi are central in Andhra Pradesh. Some festivals are celebrated in both states in their own regional style.

  • Telangana

    Bathukamma

    Floral arrangements and traditional songs. A festival that stands for Telangana's identity.

  • Telangana

    Bonalu

    Worship of Mahankali with community celebrations, processions and customs.

  • Andhra Pradesh

    Sankranti

    Bhogi, Sankranti and Kanuma, with muggulu (rangoli), harvest and family traditions.

  • Andhra Pradesh

    Ugadi

    The Telugu New Year, with traditional celebrations and family customs.

  • Both states

    Dussehra / Vijayadashami

    Good over evil. In Telangana it is celebrated statewide with family and community rites. In Andhra Pradesh the Dasara festivities at Kanaka Durga Temple, Vijayawada, are well known.

  • Both states

    Ganesh Chaturthi

    Public pandals, cultural programmes, processions and immersion, with grand events in Hyderabad.

SNIC · Dussehra period

One festival, many traditions

The camp falls in the Dussehra period. Here is how the same festival looks across India.

Telangana

Jammi tree worship, and the Bathukamma season.

Andhra Pradesh

Dasara festivities at Kanaka Durga Temple, Vijayawada.

North India

Ramlila performances and Ravana effigy burning.

South India

Mysuru Dasara in Karnataka. Golu displays in Tamil Nadu.

Eastern India

Durga Puja and Bijoya Dashami in West Bengal.

Western India

Navratri Garba and Dandiya in Gujarat.
